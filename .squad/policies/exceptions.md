# Policy: Exceptions

An exception is an explicit, temporary acceptance of a policy or gate deviation. It is not a hidden omission.

## Required content

Use the exception template to record the requirement being waived, reason, evidence, scope, risk, compensating controls, accountable risk owner, approver, start, expiry, remediation owner, and tracking reference.

## Rules

1. Only an authorized human or project-designated risk owner may approve an exception.
2. Agents may recommend but not self-authorize a waiver.
3. Exceptions are narrow, time-bound, discoverable, and re-evaluated when scope changes.
4. Expired or missing approval means the gate is not passed.
5. Emergency incident exceptions are backfilled after stabilization and receive an expiry.
6. A permanent need changes the policy/architecture through governance; it is not maintained as an endless exception.
