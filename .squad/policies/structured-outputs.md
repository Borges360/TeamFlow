# Policy: Structured Outputs

Use structure in proportion to the consumer.

- Human-only narrative may remain Markdown when required fields and evidence links are unambiguous.
- Any output parsed automatically must have a canonical representation, required fields, enum mapping and validation owned by both producer and consumer.
- Schema conformance proves structure, not factual truth, semantic completeness, authorization or business acceptance.
- Do not force verbose schemas onto exploratory communication that has no automatic consumer.
- When a Markdown representation mirrors a contract, headings or field labels must map unambiguously to the canonical fields.
