# Skill: Requirements Analysis

## Purpose

Convert a demand into observable, testable requirements.

## Procedure

1. Identify users/actors, desired outcome, current behavior, and business value.
2. Separate goals, non-goals, rules, constraints, assumptions, and questions.
3. Model primary, alternate, error, permission, boundary, and recovery scenarios.
4. Express acceptance criteria with a clear trigger, observable behavior, and outcome.
5. Add non-functional scenarios only when they are measurable or tied to project guardrails.
6. Review wording for unbounded terms such as fast, secure, scalable, intuitive, or always.
7. Trace each criterion to a stakeholder/source or mark it proposed.

## Output

Requirements and open-questions artifacts ready for the Requirement Gate.
