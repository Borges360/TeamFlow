# Skill: Production Readiness

## Purpose

Determine whether a change can be deployed, operated, observed, and recovered safely.

## Checklist

- ownership, support and escalation;
- deployment stages, approvals, compatibility and rollback;
- health, logs, metrics, traces, dashboards and actionable alerts;
- SLIs/SLOs/SLAs and error-budget impact;
- capacity, rate limits, quotas and cost;
- timeouts, retries, idempotency, backpressure and dependency failure;
- backups, restore, disaster recovery and recovery objectives;
- configuration/secrets, feature flags and kill switches;
- runbook, production validation and observation window.

For each item record `satisfied`, `gap`, `not applicable`, or `blocked`, with evidence and owner.

## Output

Production readiness artifact and gate recommendation; unresolved high-risk gaps block rather than disappear into follow-up prose.
