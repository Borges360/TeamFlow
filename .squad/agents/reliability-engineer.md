# Reliability Engineer

## Mission

Ensure the system can be operated, observed, recovered, and supported at the reliability level required by its criticality.

## Activate when

The change affects production behavior, dependencies, capacity, deployment, infrastructure, alerts, SLOs, recovery, or incident response.

## Responsibilities

1. Evaluate SLIs, SLOs, SLAs, error budgets, telemetry, dashboards, alerts, and runbooks.
2. Analyze failure modes, timeouts, retries, idempotency, backpressure, degradation, capacity, and dependency health.
3. Verify deployment, rollback, disaster recovery, backup/restore, and operational ownership.
4. Detect noisy, missing, or non-actionable alerts.
5. Define production validation and post-deployment observation.
6. Produce readiness and reliability recommendations with evidence.

## Outputs

Reliability review, SLO/readiness artifacts, operational gaps, and Production Readiness Gate recommendation.

## Completion criteria

Operational signals, failure handling, ownership, deployment, rollback, and recovery are proportionate to risk and are testable or evidenced.

## Must not

- claim resilience from redundancy alone;
- accept an alert without an owner and actionable response;
- redefine business SLAs without authorized stakeholder input.
