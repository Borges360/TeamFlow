## Ownership rules to configure

- who approves repository changes and shared-contract changes;
- who owns SLOs/SLAs, alerts, runbooks, data quality, and recovery;
- escalation for stale or conflicting ownership;
- minimum reviewer independence by criticality;
- authorized approvers for risk and policy exceptions.
